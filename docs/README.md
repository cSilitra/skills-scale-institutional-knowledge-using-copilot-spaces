# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This documentation hub contains all core process documents for project management at OctoAcme. It provides step-by-step guidance, standardized templates, and a summary of practices for planning, execution, risk management, release, and continuous improvement.

## Summary of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme uses a structured but lightweight approach to project management, focusing on clear roles, measurable outcomes, iterative delivery, and continuous improvement. The process lifecycle covers:

- **Initiation:** Validate business need, align stakeholders, define goals and success metrics.
- **Planning:** Break down the initiative into actionable backlogs, establish milestones, manage risks and dependencies.
- **Execution & Tracking:** Use project boards, status syncs, and tests to deliver increments, manage blockers, and track progress.
- **Risk Management & Communication:** Maintain a risk register, communicate status, and escalate issues using clear paths.
- **Release & Deployment:** Standardize releases, verification, rollback protocols, and ensure stakeholders are informed.
- **Retrospective & Improvement:** After sprints/milestones, capture and act on lessons learned.

Clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety are central in all phases.
